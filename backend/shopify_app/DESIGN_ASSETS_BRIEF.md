## 🎨 Required Assets

### **1. App Icon / Logo** 🔴 CRITICAL

**Requirements:**
- **Size**: 1200 × 1200 pixels
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background
- **Style**: Simple, recognizable, professional
- **Colors**: Brand colors (suggest: Blue/Purple tech colors)
- **Usage**: App store listing, app dashboard, notifications

**Design Guidelines:**
- Should work at small sizes (64x64px)
- Avoid text in the icon
- Use 2-3 colors max
- Memorable and unique

**Suggested Concepts:**
1. **Robot/AI Symbol** - Represents AI agents
2. **Product Tag Icon** - Represents catalog/taxonomy
3. **Sparkle/Magic Wand** - Represents AI enhancement
4. **Shopping Bag + AI Brain** - Combines e-commerce + AI

**Examples for Inspiration:**
- Klaviyo: Simple "K" letter
- Loox: Camera icon
- Judge.me: Star icon

### **Logo Variations Needed**

1. **Primary Logo**: Full color, light background
2. **Logo Mark Only**: Icon without text (for app icon)

4. **Monochrome**: Black and white versions

- SVG (vector)
- PNG (transparent, @1x, @2x, @3x)

### **Color Palette**

Suggested colors (adjust based on brand):

**Primary Colors:**
- **Primary Blue**: `#1976D2` (trust, tech)
- **Accent Purple**: `#7C4DFF` (AI, innovation)
- **Success Green**: `#4CAF50` (positive results)

**Secondary Colors:**
- **Warning Orange**: `#FF9800` (alerts)
- **Error Red**: `#F44336` (errors)
- **Gray**: `#616161` (text, borders)

**Background:**
- **Light**: `#FFFFFF` (primary background)
- **Surface**: `#F5F5F5` (cards, sections)
