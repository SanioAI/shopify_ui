Privacy Policy

Catalog Agents by Paladio.ai — Last updated: May 26, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Paladio.ai (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and handles data when you install and use the Catalog Agents app (“the App”) on your Shopify store.

2. Data We Collect

When you install the App, we access the following data from your Shopify store:

  • Your store URL and basic store information
  • Product data including titles, descriptions, tags, vendor, product type, and attributes

We do not collect or store personal information about your customers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we access solely to:

  • Analyze and enrich your product catalog using AI
  • Write improved product data back to your Shopify store
  • Provide support and respond to your inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

To perform AI-powered catalog enrichment, we use third-party AI infrastructure. Product data is processed solely for the purpose of generating enriched catalog content and is not retained beyond the enrichment request.

5. Data Retention

We retain store access tokens only for as long as the App is installed. When you uninstall the App, your access token is invalidated and removed from our systems.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of data we hold about your store
  • Request deletion of your store’s data
  • Uninstall the App at any time, which revokes our access

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at shopify@paladio.ai.

7. GDPR Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR. We respond to all merchant data requests and shop data deletion requests in accordance with Shopify’s requirements.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Shopify Admin API tokens are stored securely on our servers and are never exposed to the browser.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify merchants of significant changes via email.
